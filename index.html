How to play

Spin

Rotate the circle with the bezel on your watch, or swipe on your phone.

Match 3+

Three or more balls of the same colour pop. Double-tap a flying ball to drop it fast.

×8

Chain

Loose balls fall inward and line up new matches: ×2, ×3, ×5, ×8.

Special balls

Rainbow

Matches any colour. Use it to create big combos.

8

8-ball

Burns every ball it touches — even stone. The bezel catches fire when it's coming.

Stone

Balls left too long turn to stone. Only the 8-ball can break it.

FAQ

Is Bezel Pop free?

Yes. The whole game is free. The phone version shows ads between games; a one-time Premium purchase removes them.

Which watches are supported?

Wear OS 3 or newer — for example Galaxy Watch4 and later, or Pixel Watch. On Galaxy Watch Classic models you can play with the physical rotating bezel.

Do I need a rotating bezel?

No. You can always spin the circle with your finger; the bezel is simply the most satisfying way to play.

Does Premium work on both phone and watch?

Yes. Buy it once with your Google account: no ads on the phone and unlimited games on the watch.

Does it work offline?

Yes, the game itself plays offline. Ads simply don't load without a connection.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet — right now Bezel Pop is available for Android phones and Wear OS watches.
